Intelligence begins
with understanding.

Before energy can be managed,
it must be made visible.

Aura TESLA Opt. brings measurement, local computing and load control into a single wall-mounted hub. Its hardware design gives each function a clear place, and every connection a purpose.

A three-phase meter observes the supply. A fanless mini-PC provides the computing foundation. Four relay channels provide the switching layer. An Ethernet backbone connects them.

This is the V5 hardware design. Control software, commissioning and measured performance are separate work; no energy-saving percentage is claimed.
