Aura TESLA Opt.
Professional note

Architecture, scaling boundaries and local-first data handling.

FOR ENGINEERING REVIEW

1. System scope

V5 is a proposed 300 × 250 × 120 mm wall-mounted energy hub for a European 230 V / 50 Hz context. It combines three-phase measurement, local compute, four switched channels, Ethernet and a proposed 24 V UPS path. It is a design record, not a certification or commissioning instruction.

3. Industrial scalability

The architecture is modular at the site boundary: multiple hubs can be deployed per building, zone or production line, with metering and control points added at the edge. A supervisory layer may aggregate approved summaries while local hubs continue operating on the private network. Scaling requires an engineering plan for addressing, segmentation, time synchronisation, fail-safe states, thermal load, protection coordination and maintenance.

4. Local-first data boundary

With the hub on a private LAN and no cloud relay enabled, raw measurements and control traffic can remain inside the premises. The data boundary is a deployment property, not a slogan: confirm firewall rules, remote-access paths, retention, credentials, logging, backups and every third-party integration. If an external dashboard is added, only the explicitly selected data should leave the site.

5. Safety and verification

  1. Verify isolation, PE continuity, conductor sizing, protective coordination and local code compliance.
  2. Confirm enclosure thermal behaviour, IP protection and ventilation.
  3. Validate 24/12 V polarity and levels before connecting sensitive loads.
  4. Test Ethernet, Modbus and relay communications on an isolated network.
  5. Have a qualified professional apply AC and test protective-device behaviour.
