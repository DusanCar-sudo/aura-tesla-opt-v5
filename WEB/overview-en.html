THE SHORT VERSION

One wall-mounted hub to see, understand and coordinate energy.

The V5 design brings a three-phase meter, a quiet local computer, four switching channels, an Ethernet network and a proposed battery-backed power layer into one enclosure.

Its mission is to make energy visible, shift flexible loads into better tariff windows, and target 18%–32% lower avoidable energy cost in suitable operating scenarios. This is a design prediction, not independently verified field performance; it is not a finished certified product. A qualified professional must review any real installation.

WHY IT MATTERS

Mission: measurable, quieter energy.

The same pattern can be repeated across a home, workshop, commercial building or industrial site: measure at the edge, process locally, and connect only the data a wider system needs. Larger deployments can add hubs, meters and control channels without changing the basic idea.

Your data can stay inside.

Aura TESLA Opt. is designed as a local-first system. When deployed on a private network without a cloud relay, measurements and control traffic can remain inside the building. Network policy, software configuration and the chosen integrations determine the final data boundary; this page does not claim an automatic privacy guarantee.
